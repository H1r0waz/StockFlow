Later phases are planned work, not implemented features. A phase is complete only after its listed evidence is executed and recorded; missing infrastructure means **PARTIAL / NO VERIFICADO**, not a pass. No production deployment, cloud service, remote, or push is authorized by bootstrap.

## Working contract for later agents

Deliver one coherent phase/use-case slice. Re-read instructions, current schema/ADR and Git status. Assign disjoint files when delegating; coordinate shared contracts/migrations with one owner. Never reuse historical test counts as current proof. Record changed files, installed versions, exact verification commands/results, unavailable checks, data/permission limits and the next phase. Do not update the Second Brain without separate authorization and compatible permissions.

Phase 8 implements [dashboard, configured-location alerts and pagination](DASHBOARD.md) and [prospective business audit](AUDIT.md); executed evidence and limits belong to [Phase 8 verification](PHASE8_VERIFICATION.md). Previous phase reports remain historical evidence. Phase 9 implements [CSV imports](IMPORTS.md) and [reports/exports](REPORTS.md). Phase 10 adds local HTTP/error/recovery hardening and combined verification. Phase 11 provides the fictional demo, guided local visit and portfolio presentation; executed evidence belongs in [Phase 11 verification](PHASE11_VERIFICATION.md). The public portfolio demo runs on Render Free with Neon Free; see Phase 12 for URLs, exact evidence and remaining operational checks. Preserve receipt/counter/ledger/audit atomicity. Costs, returns, advanced reports and deployment remain outside current scope. External connections and deployment still require explicit authorization.
